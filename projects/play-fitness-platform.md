# PLAY Fitness Platform

## Overview

PLAY Fitness is a SaaS-oriented fitness platform concept focused on modernizing gym and fitness business operations.

## Focus Areas

- Gym management
- Customer management
- Package management
- Role-based workflows
- SaaS architecture
- Fitness business workflows
- AI-powered fitness capabilities
- Subscription and package concepts
- Reporting and business operations

## Technology

- Next.js
- React.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Prisma
- PostgreSQL
- AI

## Technical Focus

The platform is designed using a modern web application architecture with reusable frontend components, structured business workflows and scalable data models.

## AI Direction

The product architecture also explores opportunities for AI-powered fitness experiences, recommendations and intelligent business workflows.

## Project Status

Product development and architecture are evolving incrementally.
